Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to purchase the lights?

No. We supply the professional-grade lighting and normal installation materials included in your approved design.

What happens if a light stops working?

In-season maintenance for covered lighting and installation components we provide is included, subject to the service agreement.

Do you remove and store everything?

Yes. We return in January to remove the display and store the lighting for the next season.

Can you install lighting for hotels or commercial properties?

Yes. We can discuss customized installations for hospitality, retail, restaurants, vacation rentals and common areas. Projects are assessed individually.

How much does installation cost?

Every property is different. Your estimate depends on the design, installation length, access, and lighting features. Contact us for a free customized estimate.

Can I send a photo of my property?

Absolutely. Text a photo to 808-515-7579 so we can better understand your property and preferred style.
